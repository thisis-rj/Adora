06 Good questions

Before you
ask us.

Will it fit my shower?

Adora threads onto any standard half-inch shower arm — the fitting used in most Indian bathrooms. No plumber, no tools beyond your hands.

How often do I replace the cartridge?

Every 90 days, or sooner if your water is especially hard. The cartridge colours as it works, so you can see when it is spent.

Does it reduce water pressure?

No. The four stages are sized so flow stays the same as your existing showerhead.

Does it soften water?

It inactivates hardness minerals so they stop binding to your skin and hair. That is different from an ion-exchange softener, which swaps minerals for salt and needs a tank.
